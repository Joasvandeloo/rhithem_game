 Game: beat master

the player:
The player is a block with points on all sides.
The player is also on the makey makey input device.


projectiles:
Projectiles get launched at you on the beat of the music


blocking projectiles:
The player can block the projectiles with the buttons on the makey makey input device.


Songs:
there should be multiple songs.
there should be multiple rythems


home screen:
On the homescreen there are the buttons, play, settings and quit.
the play button brings you to the song selection screen.


the makey makey input device:
the device is a square with 4 buttons: UP, DOWN, RIGHT, LEFT.
if you click one of the arrows it will have 0.25 seconds to catch projectiles
