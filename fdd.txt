 Game: beat master

the player:
The player is a block with points on all sides.
The player is also on the makey makey input device.

requirements:
a makey makey,
working computer,


projectiles:
Projectiles get launched at you on the beat of the music


blocking projectiles:
The player can block the projectiles by pressing the buttons on the makey makey input device.
by pressing up, down, right or left colored arrows appear for 0.1 seconds to block the projectiles.


Songs:
there should be multiple songs of varying difficulty.
there should be multiple rhythms.


home screen:
On the home screen there are the buttons, play, settings and quit.
the play button brings you to the song selection screen then you choose a song and play.
the settings button brings you to settings.
the quit button lets you exit the game.


the makey makey input device:
the device is a square with 4 buttons: UP, DOWN, RIGHT, LEFT.
if you click one of the arrows it will have 0.1 seconds to catch projectiles.
